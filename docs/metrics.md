## 6. Known Edge Cases & System Limitations

* **Catalog gaps.** Software update, Safe mode, Dark mode, auto-rotate, font size, per-app storage and a true Factory data reset have no catalog entry, so those steps resolve to `bixby://dummy_positive` or stay manual. `DL-0022` is the *auto* factory reset, not Factory data reset.
* **Dummy rate by catalog gap.** 22 of 123 gold steps need a Settings screen the catalog lacks, and 14 are physical steps that take no link.
* **Over-cautious links.** 5 steps with a real catalog entry fell back to the placeholder because the match scored under the catalog floor: Settings > Battery > Charging; Settings > Battery and device care > Optimize now; Settings > Security and privacy > Other security settings > Pin windows; Settings > Sounds and vibration > Volume > Alarm; Settings > Sounds and vibration > Volume > Media.
* **Near-miss cache hits.** 1 of 60 near misses were served a kit answer (intent: 1): "I want to add a floating circle to my Galaxy S25 screen that gives me quick shortcuts to recent apps, home, back, screen off and volume control.".
* **Weak paraphrase registers.** typo 75% hit rate over HTTP, with the cache holding each kit query and its generated variations.
* **Paraphrases served another complaint's plan.** 5 of 180 paraphrase hits: "a16 dsplay went balck by itsself and dosnt show anyting when i try to turn it on" (row_4); "my s25 has a floating circle on the screen with shortcuts for home back and volume, how do i get rid of it" (row_12); "galaxy s26 ultra stuck boot screen small text power button" (row_15); and 2 more. The full list is in `eval/results/loadtest.json` (`paraphrase.wrong`). 17 more hit a second plan for the same complaint, made when an earlier paraphrase missed and ran cold.
* **Mismatched kit pairs.** Some kit rows pair a complaint with an article about another problem: row 7 (a dark screen, the Multi window article), row 10 (blanking at the hinge, camera flicker under lights), row 20 (a distorted screen, screen rotation) and rows 3, 11 and 17 (a garbled "things to check first" page with its words run together). The engine may only use the article, so it answers with the article's nearest fixes, and the judge marks those plans irrelevant or incomplete.
* **Free-tier variance.** Cold answers in the load test came from ministral-14b-latest 49, ministral-8b-latest 29, no model (declined, no_match) 6, rules 2. Which model answers depends on the free tier's load (a 429 puts a model on a 20 s cooldown, and past the prefer deadline the faster model's answer is taken), so the same complaint can get a different plan on another run; the cache then serves the first one identically.
* **Completeness.** The model picks at most `extract_max_actions` (8) actions per goal so a cold answer stays inside the 8 s budget on the free tier; a second, parallel call (coverage) marks the article paragraphs that help, and the ones the model skipped are added from the article's own sentences, with a numbered procedure's general-fix steps (up to `extract_complete_max_actions`, 12). Under load that call moves from 14B to 8B, which judges relevance less well. The judge marked a missing article fix on 2 of 20 plans. Critical actions come last as the spec requires, after contacting support; 0 plans were still marked with an ordering problem.
* **Multi-intent and the cache.** The cache's slot check looks at what broke, not at how many problems a complaint names, so a two-problem complaint on an article already solved for one of them can be served that one-problem plan from the semantic cache.
