## 6. Known Edge Cases & System Limitations

* **Catalog gaps.** Software update, Safe mode, Dark mode, auto-rotate, font size, per-app storage and a true Factory data reset have no catalog entry, so those steps resolve to `bixby://dummy_positive` or stay manual. `DL-0022` is the *auto* factory reset, not Factory data reset.
* **Domain gaps.** The kit's 20 complaints are all Display problems; Battery, Camera and Performance are covered only by the 15 held-out unseen scenarios (section 2). The catalog is uneven across domains: Camera has few Settings screens (most camera fixes happen inside the Camera app), so camera steps more often take `bixby://dummy_positive` or no link, and a domain the catalog barely covers gets correct steps with fewer one-tap links.
* **Settings hierarchy variations.** Menu paths differ across One UI versions and devices ("Battery" vs "Battery and device care", "Security" vs "Security and privacy"), and articles name them as the writer's phone did. Links are therefore matched on the screen's own name and description, not on the exact path the article gives, with a leaf-name boost so a parent menu does not win. Some settings exist in the catalog only as the screen that holds them, so a change with no on/off entry opens that screen's page, and only when the page is on the step's own path. Several screens have no entry in any form (see Catalog gaps).
* **Dummy rate by catalog gap.** 22 of 123 gold steps need a Settings screen the catalog lacks, and 14 are physical steps that take no link.
* **Over-cautious links.** 4 steps with a real catalog entry fell back to the placeholder because the match scored under the catalog floor: Settings > Battery > Charging; Settings > Battery and device care > Optimize now; Settings > Security and privacy > Other security settings > Pin windows; Settings > Sounds and vibration > Volume > Media.
* **Paraphrases served another complaint's plan.** 3 of 188 paraphrase hits: "my new s22 wont show anything when i power it on, not even an activation message" (row_13); "after the carier deactivted the old phoen my s22 shows nothng at al on the scren" (row_13); "galaxy s24 ultra display will not turn on device works" (row_22). The full list is in `eval/results/loadtest.json` (`paraphrase.wrong`). 16 more hit a second plan for the same complaint, made when an earlier paraphrase missed and ran cold.
* **Mismatched kit pairs.** Some kit rows pair a complaint with an article about another problem: row 7 (a dark screen, the Multi window article), row 10 (blanking at the hinge, camera flicker under lights), row 20 (a distorted screen, screen rotation) and rows 3, 11 and 17 (a garbled "things to check first" page with its words run together). The engine may only use the article, so it answers with the article's nearest fixes, and the judge marks those plans irrelevant or incomplete.
* **Free-tier variance.** Cold answers in the load test came from ministral-14b-latest 56, ministral-8b-latest 16, no model (declined, no_match) 6. Which model answers depends on the free tier's load (a 429 puts a model on a 20 s cooldown, and past the prefer deadline the faster model's answer is taken), so the same complaint can get a different plan on another run; the cache then serves the first one identically.
* **Completeness.** The model picks at most `extract_max_actions` (8) actions per goal so a cold answer stays inside the 8 s budget on the free tier; a second, parallel call (coverage) marks the article paragraphs that help, and the ones the model skipped are added from the article's own sentences, with a numbered procedure's general-fix steps (up to `extract_complete_max_actions`, 12). That call races 14B against 8B and keeps 14B's answer when it is back within 4 s; 8B judges relevance less well. The judge marked a missing article fix on 4 of 20 plans. Critical actions come last as the spec requires, after contacting support; 0 plans were still marked with an ordering problem.
* **Multi-intent and the cache.** The cache's slot check looks at what broke, not at how many problems a complaint names, so a two-problem complaint on an article already solved for one of them can be served that one-problem plan from the semantic cache.
